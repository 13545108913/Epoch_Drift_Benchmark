## Task: Draft a new marketing price rule for {{topic}} that offers {{rule}} for all customers
### Example 1 (99): Draft a new marketing price rule for fall discount that offers $10 discount on checkout for all customers
Clicked the Promotions menu to expand it and access Shopping Cart Price Rules.
```click("172")```
Clicked "Shopping Cart Price Rules" to navigate to the price rules page.
```click("179")```
Clicked the "Add New Rule..." button to create a new shopping cart price rule.
```click("447")```
Filled the rule name field with "Fall Discount $10 Off" for the new shopping cart price rule.
```fill("516", "Fall Discount $10 Off")```
Selected all customer groups in the multiselect to apply the rule to all customers.
```select_option("544", ["NOT LOGGED IN", "General", "Wholesale", "VIP Member", "Private Sales Member"])```
Clicked the "Actions" tab to access discount settings.
```click("462")```
Changed the discount type from "Percent of product price discount" to "Fixed amount discount for whole cart" to set up a $10 checkout discount.
```select_option("664", "Fixed amount discount for whole cart")```
Changed the discount amount from $0 to $10 in the discount amount field.
```fill("674", "10")```
Clicked the Save button to finalize and save the cart price rule configuration.
```click("487")```
Saved the new shopping cart price rule for a $10 fall discount for all customers.
```send_msg_to_user("Successfully created a new shopping cart price rule named 'Fall Discount $10 Off' that provides a $10 fixed discount on checkout for all customer groups.")```
